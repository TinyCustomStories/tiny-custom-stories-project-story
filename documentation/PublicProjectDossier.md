# Public Project Dossier

The downloadable [Public Project Dossier PDF](../public/documents/tiny-custom-stories-project-dossier.pdf) is a detailed public explanation of Tiny Custom Stories.

## Public scope

The dossier may cover product direction, accepted decisions, open questions, logical architecture principles, proposed workflow responsibilities, and outcome-gated delivery intent. It preserves the difference between a confirmed decision, verified fact, proposal, assumption, and open question.

## Exclusions

The dossier must not include credentials, secrets, private URLs, internal board or issue detail, private research, personal information, family or child data, exact data schemas, detailed threat analysis, operational configuration, or unpublished code.

## Maintenance

The founder reviews material public changes. A future revision must preserve knowledge-status labels, run a public-safety review, render and visually inspect every PDF page, and arrive through the public repository's protected pull-request workflow.
